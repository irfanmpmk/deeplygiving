01 About us

Pain, loss, ageing, separation, and uncertainty are inevitable parts of the human experience.

We recognise that we cannot remove life's challenges, but we can help people navigate them with greater understanding, resilience, and meaning.

Rooted in psychology and positive psychology, our purpose is to make mental health more accessible and help people enrich their human experience through life's difficulties.

01Our Philosophy

The mind can change, adapt and grow.

We believe in the mind's capacity to change, adapt, and grow. Guided by neuroplasticity and positive psychology, we focus on developing human strengths, resilience, and the conditions for lasting wellbeing and meaningful growth.

02Our Approach

Writing as a way to slow down.

Our approach centres on writing as a way to slow down, reflect, and make sense of our inner experiences. Through journalling, we encourage self-expression, emotional understanding, and the discovery of perspectives that emerge through reflection.

03Our Product

A structured space for reflection.

Grounded in positive psychology research, our journal offers a structured space for reflection, emotional exploration, and personal growth. Designed for individual use or thoughtful gifting, it helps readers make sense of their experiences and discover perspectives that may support them through life's challenges.

04 Our vision

To build a more resilient world by helping people navigate pain, recognise their protective factors, and develop the psychological resources to face life's challenges.

05 Our mission

To reach and support people through compassionate words, meaningful actions, and the power of the mind, helping them navigate life's challenges and enrich their human experience.

06 About the founders

Two friends.
One shared vision.

Founded by Dilshad K and Gopika Prasannan, two friends brought together by their background in Applied Psychology and a shared aspiration to make a meaningful difference in people's lives through psychology and wellbeing.

Originating from Malappuram and Kottayam, the founders crossed paths at the Rajiv Gandhi National Institute of Youth Development, Sriperumbudur, while pursuing their MSc in Applied Psychology. What began as an ordinary friendship gradually evolved into a shared vision for human growth and wellbeing, laying the foundation for Deeply Giving.
